Internal Reflection

Click to Play!

Internal Reflection is a two player game where the players must communicate and collaborate to complete challenges and traverse a mirrored world. The goal of the game is to capture the mysterious orb and escape this split reality.

This game was developed for the Technology Student Association (TSA) Video Game Design event. It won first place at the Colorado TSA State Conference and third place at the National TSA Conference in 2025!
